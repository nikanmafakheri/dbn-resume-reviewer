# Product Vision

## Mission

Democratize resume optimization by providing every job seeker with AI-powered, actionable feedback — regardless of their budget or network.

## Core Problems Solved

1. **ATS Black Box**: Most candidates don't know why their resume is rejected by automated systems. We provide specific ATS scores and actionable recommendations.
2. **Subjective Feedback**: Friends and family give vague advice. We provide quantified scores across multiple dimensions.
3. **Time-Consuming Iteration**: Manual resume tweaking is slow. Our instant feedback loop accelerates improvement.
4. **Inconsistent Standards**: Different recruiters value different things. Our DBN Standard system lets organizations define consistent evaluation criteria.

## Target Users

- **Primary**: Job seekers at all career levels who want to optimize their resumes
- **Secondary**: Recruiters and HR teams who want standardized resume evaluation
- **Tertiary**: Career coaches and resume writing services

## Future Vision

- Multi-resume comparison
- Industry-specific scoring models
- Real-time collaboration (share analysis with mentors)
- API for third-party integrations
- Batch processing for recruiting agencies
